The system — PARTIAL-ROT 30%

Hold TQQQ. At each month-end, if QQQ's monthly close is 5% or more below its 10-month moving average, hold 70% TQQQ and 30% Treasury bills. Otherwise hold 100% TQQQ. Trade only when the state changes — never rebalance daily.
